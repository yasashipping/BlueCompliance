Zone registry

Every MARPOL Special Area, Emission Control Area and PSSA known to the dataset, with its designation status and the date the more stringent measures took effect (MEPC.1/Circ.778/Rev.4). Geometry status tells you how the polygon was produced.

Rules

Rules are declarative data, not code. Each rule names its trigger (which zone designation, or global), the ship types it applies to, the regulation it summarises, its fact level and its sources. Edit rules.json to change behaviour.
